# Own-feed footage — provenance

> **Flag for the team: this is third-party footage, not our own camera.** It is here so
> the ANPR pipeline could be built and its recall measured against real Indian plates
> while `data/own_feed/` was empty and the government gateway was returning 502.
> **Replace it with our own recording before submission**, or cite it explicitly in the
> submission as third-party demonstration material.
